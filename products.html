Rice Vermicelli, Rice Paper & Pho Noodle Products

DARICO Food manufactures and exports premium Vietnamese rice vermicelli, Pho noodles, and rice paper in 5 natural colors — Traditional, Brown Rice, Beetroot, Gac Fruit, and Turmeric. Non-GMO, Gluten-Free, ISO 22000:2018 & HACCP certified.

Rice Vermicelli (Bun Tuoi)

Fine-strand rice vermicelli, 0.8–1.2mm, ideal for noodle bowls, salads, and fresh spring rolls.

Rice Vermicelli (Bun Bo Hue)

Thick round vermicelli, 1.5–2mm, developed for Vietnam's famous Hue-style noodle soup.

Rice Noodle (Pho)

Flat rice noodles, 3–5mm, for classic Pho and stir-fried noodle dishes.

Rice Paper (Spring Rolls)

Round & square rice paper wrappers, 16–31cm, also known as spring roll skin or spring roll wrapper.

Rice Paper (Deep Frying)

Round, square & triangle rice paper for crispy fried spring rolls (Cha Gio / frying roll).

Whether you call it rice vermicelli, rice stick, fine rice vermicelli, bihon, or bee hoon, and whether you call our wrappers rice paper, spring roll skin, spring roll wrapper, or summer roll wrapper — every DARICO product is milled and dried in Vietnam to consistent, export-ready specifications, available in 5 natural colors with no artificial dyes.
